A curated suite of minimalist, high-performance browser extensions designed for modern creators and technical professionals.

Focused browser tools for real daily workflows

This collection brings together lightweight extensions built around the small moments that slow people down: saving reusable AI prompts, moving Google Docs comments into clean project formats, and keeping media-heavy interfaces comfortable during work or study. Each tool is designed to do one job clearly, stay close to the page where the work happens, and avoid unnecessary account setup or server-side processing.

Spark helps writers, developers, students, and teams keep their best AI prompts organized so useful instructions are easy to find again. Docs Comment Exporter turns review discussions into Markdown, JSON, or CSV for documentation handoffs, audits, release notes, and research workflows. YouTube Music SFW Mode makes background listening more private and less distracting by masking artwork without changing the core music experience.

The site is intentionally simple: fast-loading product pages, direct demo links, clear feature summaries, and screenshots that show the tools in context. The goal is to help visitors understand what each extension does, decide whether it fits their workflow, and move from discovery to use without wading through marketing clutter.
