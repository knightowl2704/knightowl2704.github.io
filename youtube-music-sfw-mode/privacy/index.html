Privacy Policy

YouTube Music SFW Mode

Your privacy is a top priority for YouTube Music SFW Mode. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our extension.

1. Data Collection

YouTube Music SFW Mode does not collect, store, track, or share any of your personal information.

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • We do not collect web history, browsing activity, or user activity.
  • We do not collect or transmit any website content, including images, text, or music data, to any external servers.

2. Local Data Storage

The extension uses the Chrome storage API to save your personal preferences locally on your own computer. This includes:

  • Your selected masking mode (Blur, Solid, or Initials).
  • Your chosen blur intensity settings.
  • Your preference for the "Hover-to-Peek" feature.

This information stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any third-party servers.

3. Permissions

This extension requires access to music.youtube.com. This permission is used exclusively to modify the visual presentation of the website on your local browser (masking album artwork). The extension does not use this permission to interact with your personal data, accounts, or browsing activity outside of the YouTube Music interface.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or transfer any data to third parties. We do not use user data for any purposes unrelated to the extension’s single function: providing a distraction-free listening environment.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
