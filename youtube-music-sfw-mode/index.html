Add to Chrome – Free Watch Demo

Take control of your workspace privacy

Everything you need to listen in peace, right in your browser.

🛡️

Dynamic Masking Modes

Customize how artwork is hidden with three distinct styles: Blur Mode softly blurs the artwork to keep ambient colors, Solid Mode blocks the image with a matched color scheme, and Initials Mode replaces covers with song or artist initials.

👁️

Hover-to-Peek (Curiosity Mode)

Want to see the album cover for a split second? Simply hover your mouse over any masked image to temporarily reveal the original artwork. The moment you move your mouse away, it’s instantly hidden again.

🎛️

Adjustable Blur Intensity

Take full control of your privacy. Use the slider in the control panel to choose anything from a light, semi-transparent blur to a completely opaque mask.

⚡

Emergency Keyboard Shortcut

Toggle SFW Protection mode instantly using Alt + Shift + B. No need to click around when a coworker or boss approaches your desk.
