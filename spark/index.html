Pin Spark for instant access

To open your prompt library anytime you need it:

  1. Click the puzzle icon (🧩) in the top-right corner of your browser and pin (📌) Spark.
  2. Click the Spark icon (⚡) in your browser toolbar, then click Allow access in the popup to enable Spark on the current page.
  3. Refresh the page. You will now see the floating Spark button on the right side of your screen. Click it to open your prompt library!

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Spark? We have answers.

How do I save and organize my ChatGPT prompts for later use?

Spark lets you save prompts from anywhere and organize them instantly. You can build custom folders, apply color-coding, and favorite your top snippets so you can retrieve the exact command you need in seconds. The extension stores all prompts locally in your browser with zero external tracking.

How do I auto-paste saved prompts into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini?

Spark provides a 1-Click Auto-Paste feature that injects templates directly into active input fields on any AI platform. If a site isn't fully compatible, a manual copy button is also available to ensure universal website compatibility.

Can I export my prompt library and import it to a new device?

Yes. Spark lets you download your entire library into a secure backup file with a single click. You can then import it on a new device to instantly migrate your workflows. This is perfect for switching browsers or setting up a new computer.

Is my prompt data stored on external servers or shared with third parties?

No. Spark stores all text locally in your browser with zero external tracking, telemetry, or data collection. Your proprietary prompts and templates remain completely secure and private.

Does Spark work with AI platforms other than ChatGPT?

Yes. Spark is platform-agnostic. It works on most of the AI websites with a text input field. The sidebar stays accessible whether you're on chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, and a lot more. If it does not work, feel free to use the 'Contact Us' link and let us know. We will add support for it. The auto-paste feature intelligently detects the active input field on these major platforms and injects your prompt with one click.
